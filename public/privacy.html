Privacy policy

Shiftly · Last updated 25 October 2026

Shiftly helps you track your shifts and estimate your pay. This policy explains what information the app keeps, where it is stored and how you can delete it.

What Shiftly stores

  • Your account: your email address and password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are never visible to us.
  • What you enter: your jobs and pay rates, shifts, ABN earnings, payslip amounts, goals and app settings.

Shiftly does not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not collect your location, contacts or any other information from your device.

Where it is stored

Your data is kept on your device so the app works offline. When you are signed in it is also synced to a secure cloud database (Supabase) so it is available on your other devices. Data is encrypted in transit, and each account can only ever read its own data. The app itself is hosted by Vercel.

Who can see it

Only you. Your information is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and is used only to run the app for you. The service providers above store it on our behalf and may not use it for anything else.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account and everything synced to it, straight away. Signing out removes the copy on that device.

Pay figures are estimates

Shiftly calculates pay, tax and super from the rates and rules you enter. These are estimates to help you plan and check your pay — always compare them with your payslip, and contact your employer or the ATO about any differences.

Children

Shiftly is meant for working people and is not directed at children under 13.
